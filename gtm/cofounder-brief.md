# Cofounder brief — Custos

*The document you send when someone asks "so what are you working on?" Written to recruit, honestly.*

## The one-liner

Custos is the trust layer for AI agent payments: cryptographic verification of who operates an agent, hard bounds on what it can spend, and a risk score on every transaction it makes. The Stripe Radar + Plaid of the agent economy.

## Why this, why now

AI agents are transacting — 165M+ x402 transactions, with Coinbase, Google, Visa, and Stripe all shipping agent-payment rails — but average transaction value is ~$0.30 because nobody can answer three questions: *who runs this agent, what may it spend, and who pays when it goes wrong?* In every prior payments shift, the rails commoditized and the neutral risk layer captured the margin (Radar, Sift, Forter, Plaid). The agent-economy risk layer is currently unowned. Full plan, including the adversarial critique and honest odds: `docs/business-plan.md`.

## What's true today (no varnish)

- Stage: pre-incorporation, pre-funding, solo founder.
- Done: full business plan iterated against simulated partner-level critique; market-validation research in flight; design-partner outreach kit ready; KYA spec and prototype next.
- The bet: genuine agent-commerce volume grows 10–50× by 2028. If it does, this is a $1B+ company; honest estimate ~4–7% (vs ~1% seed base rate). Pre-committed kill criterion: if genuine protected volume is under $3M/month by month 12, we pivot the risk engine to stablecoin B2B fraud — a live adjacent market.
- Biggest risks, stated plainly: market 24 months early; platform bundling by Coinbase/Stripe/Google; two years of small-SaaS-looking revenue.
